# Code of Conduct

OpenAgents World aims to keep collaboration useful, safe, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their merits.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's credentials or personal information.
- Credit sources and document asset licenses.
- Help keep discussions welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, doxxing, spam, impersonation, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [contact@openagentsworld.com](mailto:contact@openagentsworld.com). Project maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
